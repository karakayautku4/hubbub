# IP and trademark notes

Hubbub is MIT-licensed and is intended to be published openly. This page records what is original, what is not, and
the rules contributors follow so that it stays that way. It is a project policy document, not legal advice.

## What is original

- **All 3D scenes, characters, props, textures and sounds are procedural.** They are built in code from SceneKit
  primitives, generated bitmaps (`Gfx.swift`) and synthesised audio. There are no bundled models, images, fonts,
  music or sound samples taken from other works. The only bundled bitmaps are the app icon and notification icon in `Resources/`.
- **Theme names, jokes, speech lines, job names, rank titles and prop names are written for this project.** Themes are
  genre parodies (space station, kitchen, prison, football, farm, spaceship whodunit, wizard academy, space fleet,
  fantasy road trip). A genre or a trope is not owned by anyone; specific names, catchphrases and designs are.
- **Football uses fictional clubs.** The ten clubs in `Team.swift` (Crimson Foxes, Sunflower Rovers, Ink Magpies, ...)
  and their generic kit colours are invented. No real club, league, competition, player, sponsor or chant appears.
  Any resemblance is coincidental. Generic football vocabulary (referee, red card, VAR, offside) is used as plain English.

## Policy for theme content

Do not add:

- names, logos, crests, kits or mascots of real organisations, clubs, brands, products or people;
- character, place, house, faction, spell, ship or item names that come from a film, book, game or show
  (including thinly altered spellings), or their signature catchphrases and quotes;
- recognisable copies of a protected design (silhouettes, colour schemes plus names, costumes, logos);
- audio or image samples you did not create or that are not under a licence compatible with MIT.

Generic slang and common words are fine (for example "sus", "red alert", "wand", "potion", "tavern", "droid-like
helper bots" described as "bots"). Aim for **original jokes in the spirit of the genre** rather than references.
Community JSON themes you publish yourself are your responsibility; the same rules apply to anything merged here.
